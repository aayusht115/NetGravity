Contract Reference: SF-2026-1180

Effective Date:  01 April 2026
Expiry Date:     31 March 2027

1. RATE

1.1 All-Inclusive Rate
SpeedFreight India Limited shall carry all consignments at a flat, all-
inclusive rate of Rs. 12.00 per kg.

1.2 Inclusions
The rate stated in Clause 1.1 is inclusive of fuel surcharge, handling,
documentation and last-mile delivery. No additional surcharge of any nature
shall be levied.

2. COVERAGE

2.1 Serviceable Area
SpeedFreight India Limited services all pin codes within the territory of
India, including the North Eastern states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and the
island territories. There are no non-serviceable locations under this
Agreement.

3. MINIMUM CONSIGNMENT
A minimum consignment weight of 200 kg per shipment shall apply.

4. PAYMENT TERMS
Invoices shall be raised monthly and are payable within 30 days.
