================================================================
TRANSCORP LOGISTICS PRIVATE LIMITED
MASTER FREIGHT SERVICES AGREEMENT
Contract Reference: TC-2026-0472
================================================================

Effective Date:  01 April 2026
Expiry Date:     31 March 2027

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
TransCorp Logistics Private Limited ("the Carrier") shall provide surface
transportation services for consumer durable goods across the Client's
distribution network within the territory of India.

2. RATE SCHEDULE

2.1 Base Freight Rate
The base freight rate shall be Rs. 10.00 per kg for all consignments
tendered under this Agreement, subject to the adjustments in Clause 3.

2.2 Minimum Consignment
A minimum consignment weight of 500 kg per shipment shall apply. Consignments
below this weight shall be charged at the 500 kg minimum.

3. SURCHARGES AND ADJUSTMENTS

3.1 Fuel Surcharge
A fuel surcharge of Rs. 2.00 per kg shall apply to all consignments
irrespective of destination. This surcharge shall be reviewed quarterly with
reference to the PPAC retail selling price index.

3.2 Non-Serviceable Location Handling Charge
Deliveries to Non-Serviceable Locations (Annexure C, 12 pin codes in the
North East region) attract an additional handling charge of Rs. 5.00 per kg.
Non-Serviceable Locations are those beyond the Carrier's owned network, where
delivery is effected through an appointed sub-contractor.

4. SERVICE LEVELS AND PENALTIES

4.1 Late Pickup
A penalty of Rs. 500 per incident shall be payable by the Carrier where
pickup is not effected within the agreed window.

5. PAYMENT TERMS
Invoices shall be raised fortnightly and are payable within 45 days.
