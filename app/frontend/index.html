    How this scenario is solved
    Objective: minimise total network cost - transport, handling, fixed facility, inventory and carbon.
    Subject to: each market's stated service level (a lane slower than the promise is removed before the solve), every facility's stated capacity, and lane capacities where the upload states them.
    Solver: PuLP / HiGHS branch-and-cut.
